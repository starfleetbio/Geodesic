## Notes
- Panels are pre-oriented flat, outer-face-down; leave them as-is (don't let auto-orient re-tilt them).
- T3-L and T3-R share grey3 but are different shapes — separate plates, same spool.
- Clear arcs are packed 2/plate with room to spare — let Bambu auto-arrange consolidate the 10 arcs onto ~2 plates.
- Filament = solid volume × 1.24 g/cm³; add ~10–20% for brim/purge. Slice one plate for a real time estimate.
- **Before the full run:** print T1_plate1, drop real 1/8" magnets into a few sockets, confirm the Ø1.4 window + 3.1×0.8 neck hold and the ball spins.
